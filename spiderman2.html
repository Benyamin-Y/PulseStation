Marvel's Spider-Man 2

Be Greater. Together. Experience the incredible power of Peter Parker and Miles Morales as they face the ultimate test.

Swing In

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is an action-adventure game developed by Insomniac Games and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment for PlayStation 5. In this exciting sequel, players can switch between Peter Parker and Miles Morales as they protect New York City from dangerous villains. The game features a larger open world, faster web-swinging, intense combat, and an emotional story filled with friendship, responsibility, and sacrifice. With stunning graphics, smooth gameplay, and memorable boss battles, Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is considered one of the best superhero games ever created.

Trailer

Peter Parker

Peter Parker is one of the most famous superheroes in the Marvel Universe and one of the main protagonists in Marvel's Spider-Man 2. After gaining extraordinary spider-like abilities, Peter dedicates his life to protecting New York City from dangerous criminals and powerful supervillains. He is known for his intelligence, bravery, and strong sense of responsibility, always putting the safety of others before himself. Throughout the game, Peter faces some of his greatest challenges, including the arrival of the mysterious Symbiote, which grants him incredible new powers but slowly influences his emotions and behavior. As the story unfolds, Peter must overcome both powerful enemies and his own inner struggles, proving that true heroism comes from courage, compassion, and the willingness to do what is right, no matter the cost.

Miles Morales

Miles Morales is the younger Spider-Man who gained his powers after being bitten by a genetically enhanced spider. Under Peter Parker's guidance, Miles quickly developed into a formidable hero dedicated to protecting his home in Brooklyn and all of New York City. In addition to standard spider abilities, Miles possesses unique powers, including bio-electric Venom blasts and camouflage invisibility. As he faces new threats alongside Peter, Miles must learn to balance his high school life, family responsibilities, and his duty as a protector, proving that anyone can wear the mask and make a difference.

Mary Jane

Mary Jane "MJ" Watson is an investigative journalist for the Daily Bugle and Peter Parker's partner. In Marvel's Spider-Man 2, MJ plays a crucial active role, using her bravery, intelligence, and a modified stun gun to sneak behind enemy lines and gather vital clues. Her determination to help Peter and Miles while uncovering the truth makes her an essential part of the team.

Venom & Kraven

In Marvel's Spider-Man 2, Peter and Miles face two of their greatest threats yet: Kraven the Hunter and the fearsome Symbiote, Venom. Kraven arrives in New York City with his faction of hunters, turning the city into his personal hunting grounds to challenge Earth's most powerful beings. Meanwhile, the alien Symbiote binds with a host, giving rise to Venom—a monstrous, unstoppable force driven by anger and power. Together, the two Spider-Men must combine their strength and skills to save their city from total destruction.

Lizard

The Lizard is one of the most dangerous enemies in Marvel’s Spider-Man 2. His real name is Dr. Curt Connors, a brilliant scientist who becomes a huge reptile-like creature after a failed experiment. With incredible strength, sharp claws, and powerful healing abilities, The Lizard becomes a serious threat to Spider-Man. Behind his monster appearance, Curt Connors is still a good person who struggles to control his transformation and find a way to return to his human form.

Norman Osborn

Norman Osborn is the billionaire CEO of Oscorp and Harry's father. Driven by desperation to save his dying son, Norman takes extreme measures that inadvertently unleash powerful threats upon New York City. His protective instincts as a father and his immense influence make him a key political and narrative figure in the Spider-Man universe.

Harry Osborn

Harry Osborn is Peter Parker's best friend and the son of Norman Osborn. After spending years in medical isolation to treat a terminal illness using the alien Symbiote, Harry returns with a vision to heal the world alongside Peter. However, his deep bond with the Symbiote takes a dark turn, making his tragic story the emotional core of Marvel's Spider-Man 2.

Black Cat

Felicia Hardy, also known as Black Cat, is a master thief and an unpredictable ally to Spider-Man. Possessing incredible agility, combat skills, and expert acrobatics, Felicia always walks the fine line between hero and criminal. In Marvel's Spider-Man 2, Miles Morales encounters her during a high-stakes chase through New York where she utilizes a magical artifact to escape Kraven's hunters, proving once again that she is always one step ahead of everyone else.
