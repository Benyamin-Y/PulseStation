Grand Theft Auto VI

Welcome back to Vice City.

Explore Now

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally taking us back to Vice City, and honestly, it looks absolutely wild. From the bright beaches and busy streets to the crazy stuff happening all over the map, this feels like a huge step up from the GTA we've played before. Rockstar has been keeping things pretty quiet, but every trailer gives us more reasons to be excited.

GTA 6

GTA 6 is the next big game from Rockstar Games and the newest entry in the Grand Theft Auto series. This time we're heading back to Vice City and following Jason and Lucia as they try to make their way through a world full of crime, money, and trouble. It's an open-world game, so you can follow the story or just forget about everything and drive around causing chaos for hours.

Price & Release Date

📅 Release Date Nov 19, 2026

Editions

GTA 6 launches in two editions. The core story is identical in both — Ultimate just adds a pile of exclusive extras woven into the campaign.

Standard Edition

$79.99

  • Full GTA 6 story experience
  • Vintage Vice City Pack (pre-order bonus)
  • Can upgrade to Ultimate anytime later
Most Content

Ultimate Edition

$99.99

  • Everything in Standard
  • 5 exclusive vehicles
  • 4 exclusive weapon variants
  • 5 exclusive shops (incl. hair salon)
  • 2 side missions

Jason & Lucia

Jason and Lucia are the main characters of GTA 6, and they seem to have a pretty interesting relationship. They're both trying to make money and survive in a world where things can go wrong really fast. From what we've seen, they trust each other a lot, but being partners in crime probably isn't going to be the easiest thing in the world. Their story looks like it's going to be one of the biggest parts of the game.

Characters

Jason and Lucia aren't the only interesting people in GTA 6. We've already seen several strange and memorable characters in the trailers, and knowing Rockstar, some of them are probably going to be completely insane. GTA has always been good at creating characters that you remember long after finishing the game, so I'm really curious to see who ends up becoming the fan favorite this time.

Vice city

Vice City is back, but this isn't the same city we saw in the older GTA game. Rockstar has made it feel much bigger and more alive, with beaches, highways, neighborhoods, clubs, and huge areas outside the city. There always seems to be something going on, whether you're driving through town, hanging around the beach, or just watching random people do their thing. It looks like a place where you could get distracted for hours without even touching a mission.

The World

One of the coolest things about GTA 6 is how alive the world looks. People aren't just standing around waiting for you to walk past them — they're doing their own things, taking photos, hanging out, driving, partying, and getting into random situations. The map also goes way beyond the main city, so there's a lot more to explore than just Vice City itself.

Gameplay

The gameplay seems to have everything you'd expect from GTA, but with a lot more detail packed into it. You can drive around, explore the map, use different weapons, take part in missions, and just mess around with the world whenever you feel like it. Rockstar also seems to be putting a lot of effort into making the characters, cars, animations, and environments feel more natural, which should make simply walking around the world more fun.

Vehicles

Cars have always been a huge part of GTA, and GTA 6 looks like it's going to have a pretty crazy collection of vehicles. From sports cars and regular street cars to motorcycles and boats, there's going to be plenty of ways to get around Vice City. And let's be honest, half the fun is probably going to be stealing a random car and seeing how long you can keep it before everything goes wrong.

Weapons

GTA 6 wouldn't really feel like GTA without a bunch of weapons to mess around with. We've already seen different types of firearms in the trailers, and there will probably be plenty more hidden throughout the game. Whether you're following a mission or just causing trouble around Vice City, having the right weapon is definitely going to make a difference.

Trailers

The GTA 6 trailers have already shown us a lot about the game, including Jason and Lucia, Vice City, different characters, vehicles, and some of the crazy situations we'll probably get ourselves into. But Rockstar is still keeping plenty of things hidden, so every new trailer or piece of information gets people trying to figure out what's actually coming.
