God of War Ragnarök

Embark on an epic and heartfelt journey as Kratos and Atreus struggle with holding on and letting go.

Explore Realm

Kratos and Atreus must journey to each of the Nine Realms in search of answers as Asgardian forces prepare for a prophesied battle that will end the world. Along the way, they will explore stunning, mythical landscapes, and face fearsome enemies in the form of Norse gods and monsters. The threat of Ragnarök grows ever closer.

Trailer

Who is Kratos?

Kratos is the legendary main character of the God of War series. Once a brutal Spartan warrior tricked into serving the Greek gods, he destroyed Olympus in a quest for revenge. Years later, he travels to the frozen north, seeking a quiet life. In Midgard, Kratos becomes a father again to Atreus. Now, he must fight his dark past to protect his son from the Norse gods and the arrival of Ragnarök.

Atreus

Atreus is the son of Kratos and Faye, and a key character in the modern God of War Norse saga. Known by the giants as Loki, he is a young warrior torn between his human emotions, Spartan heritage, and godhood. Atreus is an expert archer and possesses a magical ability to understand ancient languages and speak with animals. As Ragnarök approaches, he seeks to discover his true identity and destiny, trying to find a peaceful path to save the realms while keeping a strong but complicated bond with his father.

THOR

Thor is one of the main characters and powerful enemies in God of War Ragnarök. He is the legendary Norse God of Thunder and the son of Odin, the ruler of Asgard. Thor is feared across the Nine Realms because of his incredible strength and his magical hammer, Mjölnir, which can summon lightning and always returns to his hand after being thrown. Unlike the heroic version seen in many movies, the God of War version of Thor is portrayed as a ruthless and dangerous warrior who has spent years fighting for Odin. Despite his brutal reputation, Thor also struggles with his past, his family, and the pressure of living up to Odin's expectations. Throughout the story, his encounters with Kratos become some of the most intense and memorable battles in the game, making him one of the most complex and unforgettable characters in the Norse saga.

Odin

Odin is the main antagonist of God of War Ragnarök and the ruler of Asgard. Known as the All-Father, he is one of the most powerful and intelligent gods in Norse mythology. Unlike many warriors, Odin prefers to use his wisdom, manipulation, and knowledge to achieve his goals rather than relying only on brute strength. He is obsessed with discovering the secrets of the future and preventing his own death during Ragnarök. Throughout the story, Odin manipulates both gods and mortals to serve his plans, making him a dangerous and unpredictable enemy. His conflict with Kratos and Atreus plays a central role in the game's story, and his actions shape the fate of the Nine Realms.

Freya

Freya is one of the most important characters in God of War Ragnarök. She is the former Queen of the Valkyries, a powerful Vanir goddess, and the mother of Baldur. After the death of her son, Freya blames Kratos and vows to take revenge. However, as the story progresses, she begins to understand the truth and eventually becomes one of Kratos' strongest allies. Freya is highly skilled in magic, healing, and combat, making her one of the most powerful characters in the Nine Realms. Her journey is one of grief, forgiveness, and redemption, and she plays a crucial role in the battle against Odin during Ragnarök.
